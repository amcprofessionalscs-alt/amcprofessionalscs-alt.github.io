Start your CBRF career.

More than a certificate. Training, a uniform, real practice in a home, and help finding work.

May be covered by workforce funding (WIOA) if you qualify. Ask your Job Center or Employ Milwaukee caseworker.

Everything included

What you get

  • All 4 required CBRF courses: Standard Precautions, Fire Safety, First Aid and Choking (with CPR-AED), and Medication Administration
  • Resident Rights
  • Wisconsin's 15-hour training for 1–2 bed adult family homes
  • Your registry fee
  • Uniforms and supplies
  • On-the-job practice in a real care setting
  • Help finding work in a home after you finish

Is it free?

It may be. Our program is on Wisconsin's Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL). People who qualify for workforce funding (WIOA) through their Job Center can use it here.

Your caseworker decides if you qualify.

How it works

Five steps

  1. Call us. We'll tell you when the next class starts.
  2. Talk to your caseworker. If you work with a Job Center or Employ Milwaukee, ask about workforce funding for “All For You CBRF Training.” We can help with the paperwork.
  3. Come to class. About 20 hours over one week, in person, during the day.
  4. Practice in a home. Hands-on time in a real care setting.
  5. Get on the registry and get to work. When you pass, you're added to the Wisconsin registry, and we help you connect with homes that are hiring.
